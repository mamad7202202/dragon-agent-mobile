Providers & models

Dragon speaks two protocols: OpenAI-compatible (most of the ecosystem) and Anthropic-native. Pick a preset and the URL, protocol and model suggestions are set for you.

switchChanging models mid-chat

Tap the model chip in the top bar. The sheet lists your provider's models plus a free-text field for any id. The switch applies from the next message; the transcript stays untouched.

thinkDeep thinking

Settings → Intelligence → Deep thinking sets the reasoning effort:

  • OpenAI-compatible — the request carries reasoning_effort: low | medium | high. Gemini 2.5+, OpenAI reasoning models and OpenRouter honour it; models without reasoning ignore it safely.
  • Anthropic — a real thinking budget: 2k, 8k or 16k tokens of extended thinking.

When the model thinks, a golden Thinking… pill grows above the answer. Tap it to read the raw reasoning.

searchWeb search

Toggle in Settings → Intelligence. It's wired natively, not faked: OpenRouter appends :online to the model; Anthropic enables its server-side web_search tool (up to three searches per turn). Providers without native search say so instead of pretending.

tokensUsage, per message

When a provider reports usage, each answer gets a small 1.4k tokens badge next to the copy button. That's it — no dashboards, no nagging.
