Providers & models

Dragon speaks two protocols: OpenAI-compatible (used by most of the ecosystem) and Anthropic-native. Pick a preset and the protocol, URL and suggestions are configured for you.

Switching models mid-chat

Tap the model chip in the top bar — a sheet lists your provider's models plus a free-text field for any custom id. The switch applies from the next message; history stays intact.

Deep thinking (reasoning)

Settings → Intelligence → Deep thinking:

  • OpenAI-compatible providers receive reasoning_effort: low | medium | high. Gemini 2.5+, OpenAI o-series/gpt-5 class and OpenRouter reasoning models honour it; others ignore it safely.
  • Anthropic gets a real thinking budget (2k / 8k / 16k tokens) with extended thinking enabled.

When the model thinks, a golden “Thinking…” pill appears above the answer — tap it to read the raw thought process.

Web search

Toggle in Settings → Intelligence. Implemented natively per provider:

  • OpenRouter — appends :online to the model.
  • Anthropic — enables the server-side web_search tool (up to 3 searches per turn).

The toggle is disabled with an explanation for providers without native search support.

Token usage

When the provider reports usage, each answer shows a subtle ⚡ 1.4k tokens badge next to the copy button — no clutter, just awareness.
